# UI/UX & WhatsApp Conversion Skill

## Conversion Heuristics for The Garage
1. **Above-the-Fold Actionability**: On mobile screens (375px–420px), the bike photo, title, price in MXN, availability badge, and primary WhatsApp CTA must be instantly visible without scrolling.
2. **Deep-Linked Pre-Filled WhatsApp Messages**: Every bike card and detail page must generate a contextual message mentioning the exact brand, model, and size (`https://wa.me/529841381493?text=...`).
3. **Availability & Social Proof**: Sold items (`Vendida`) remain visible with a muted grayscale badge to generate social proof and urgency.
4. **Persistent Floating CTA**: Global WhatsApp button positioned at bottom-right with high contrast and accessible tap target (minimum 48x48px).
5. **Caribbean Outdoor Readability**: High-contrast typography (#18181B on #FFFFFF and #F8FAFC) that remains readable under intense outdoor sunlight.
